Hi! I am **Youngseon Kim**, an M.S. student in Computer Engineering at **Chung-Ang University**.

My research focuses on **multimodal learning, computer vision, and vision-language models**, with particular interests in video understanding, multimodal reasoning, and retrieval-augmented generation.

### Research Interests

- Multimodal Learning
- Vision-Language Models
- Video Understanding
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation
- Computer Vision
- Multimodal Reasoning

I am particularly interested in developing multimodal systems that can effectively integrate visual information with external knowledge for real-world video understanding.
