SetPieceRAG

Domain-Specific RAG for Knowledge-Intensive Soccer VQA with Large Language Models

Youngseon Kim · Jongmin Lee
Chung-Ang University
Paper ↗ Poster ↗ Portfolio

Overview

Soccer video question answering often requires information that is not visible in the broadcast frame itself: player identity, career history, team context, and historical statistics. SetPieceRAG combines visual grounding with domain-specific retrieval so that a model can connect what it sees to the external knowledge needed to answer.

Method

01

Visual Grounding

CLIP-based retrieval identifies the relevant player or visual entity from the query image.

02

Domain Retrieval

SoccerWiki and web knowledge provide facts that cannot be inferred reliably from pixels alone.

03

Task-Adaptive Routing

Lightweight generation handles grounded cases while fallback routing escalates harder questions when needed.

Why Retrieval Matters

The test analyses show the central failure mode clearly: an image-only model can recognize visual cues but often cannot answer questions about a player's youth clubs, career statistics, transfer history, or historical appearances. Retrieved domain knowledge supplies the missing evidence, while routing prevents every query from paying the cost of the strongest model.
